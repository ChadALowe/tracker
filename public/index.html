The field

Every minute someone is unavailable, their horse gallops. Available or Busy? It stands still. The leader crosses the line when tracking hours end.

The horse in front sits at the race clock; everyone else is placed by how their unavailable time compares to the leader's.

🌴 On leave

People on leave sit out the race on those days, and leave days don't count towards any totals.

The rules

  1. Time only counts during tracking hours. Your Cloudflare Worker checks everyone's Teams status every minute, in a single request, during the hours set in Settings (UK time). No browser needs to be open. Outside those hours, or when tracking is stopped for the day, nothing is counted.
  2. Each gap between checks takes the earlier status. If a check at 10:00 says Available and the next at 10:01 says Away, that minute counts as Available. Totals can be out by up to one check interval per status change.
  3. Long gaps are skipped. If more than 3 minutes passes between checks (a missed run, Microsoft not answering), the gap isn't counted. The limit is 2× the check interval or 3 minutes, whichever is larger.
  4. Offline isn't online. Time showing as Offline or Unknown is left out of every total.
  5. "Available" and "Busy" count as available. Busy covers being in a call or meeting. Every other online status — Busy (idle), Do not disturb, Be right back, Away, Available (idle) — counts as unavailable.
  6. The Away adjustment. Teams only marks someone Away after about 5 minutes of inactivity. When a person goes from Available or Busy straight to Away (or an idle/inactive status), the last 5 minutes of that stretch are re-labelled as unavailable. It never reaches back past the start of the stretch the monitor saw. On timelines this shows as a striped block.
  7. Leave doesn't count. Days someone is marked on leave (Race page → On leave) are left out of every total, timeline and export for that person, even if Teams showed them online.
  8. Failed checks aren't guessed. If a check for someone fails, nothing is counted for them until their next successful check.
  9. Periods. Today runs from midnight on the clock of the device you're viewing on. Last 7 days is today plus the previous 6 days. All time is everything kept. Time crossing midnight is split between the two days. History older than 180 days is deleted automatically.

Worked example

Tracking starts at 09:00 and Sam is Available. Sam steps away at 09:15; Teams marks Sam Away at about 09:20 and the next check sees it. Sam is Available again from 09:30. Tracking ends at 10:00.

Online 09:00–10:00 = 60 min · Unavailable 09:20–09:30 (10 min) + 5-minute Away adjustment (09:15–09:20) = 15 min · Available 60 − 15 = 45 min (75%).

If checks had failed from 09:40 to 09:50, those 10 minutes would simply be missing — Online 50 min, Available 35 min — and on the timeline they'd show as an empty striped gap. Unmonitored time is never counted as Available.

Connection

One-time Microsoft setup

  1. In the Microsoft Entra admin center, open your app registration (the existing one is fine).
  2. Under Certificates & secrets, create a client secret and copy its Value.
  3. Under API permissions, add Microsoft Graph Application permissions Presence.Read.All and User.Read.All, then Grant admin consent.
  4. In Cloudflare, add Worker secrets AZURE_TENANT_ID, AZURE_CLIENT_ID, AZURE_CLIENT_SECRET and PORTAL_PASSWORD.
