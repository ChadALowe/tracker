The rules

  1. Time only counts while the monitor is running. The app checks each tracked person's Teams status every 60 seconds. If this page is closed, reloaded, or you press Stop, nothing is counted until you press Start again.
  2. Each gap between checks takes the earlier status. If a check at 10:00 says Available and the next at 10:01 says Busy, the minute from 10:00 to 10:01 counts as Available. Totals can therefore be out by up to one check interval per status change.
  3. Long gaps are skipped. If more than 3 minutes passes between two checks (computer asleep, network down, browser paused the tab), that gap is not counted at all. This is the larger of 2× the check interval or 3 minutes.
  4. Offline isn't online. Time when someone shows as Offline or Unknown is left out of every total.
  5. Only "Available" counts as available. Every other online status — Busy, In a call, Do not disturb, Be right back, Away, Available (idle) — counts as unavailable.
  6. The Away adjustment. Teams only switches someone to Away after about 5 minutes without activity. So when a person goes straight from Available to Away (or an idle/inactive status), the 5 minutes before are moved from Available to Unavailable. It never moves more than their latest unbroken Available stretch that the monitor saw.
  7. Failed checks aren't guessed. If a check for someone fails, the time from that check until their next successful one isn't counted.
  8. Totals are cumulative, not daily. They keep adding up across days and sessions until you use Reset totals & log.

Worked example

The monitor is started at 09:00 and Sam is Available. Sam steps away at 09:15. Teams marks Sam Away at about 09:20, and the next check sees it. Sam comes back and is Available again from 09:30. The monitor is stopped at 10:00.

Online: 09:00–10:00 = 60 min.   Unavailable: 09:20–09:30 (10 min) + the 5-minute Away adjustment = 15 min.   Available: 60 − 15 = 45 min (75%).

If the page had been closed from 09:40 to 09:50, those 10 minutes would simply be missing: Online 50 min, Available 35 min. They are never counted as Available.
