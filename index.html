The rules

  1. Time only counts while the monitor is live. This page checks everyone's Teams status every 60 seconds, in a single request. Keep the tab open — it can sit in the background. If it's closed, reloaded or stopped, nothing is counted until the monitor goes live again.
  2. Each gap between checks takes the earlier status. If a check at 10:00 says Available and the next at 10:01 says Busy, that minute counts as Available. Totals can be out by up to one check interval per status change.
  3. Long gaps are skipped. If more than 3 minutes passes between checks (computer asleep, network down), the gap isn't counted. The limit is 2× the check interval or 3 minutes, whichever is larger. Browsers slow background tabs to about one check a minute, which stays within this limit.
  4. Offline isn't online. Time showing as Offline or Unknown is left out of every total.
  5. Only "Available" counts as available. Every other online status — Busy, In a call, Do not disturb, Be right back, Away, Available (idle) — counts as unavailable.
  6. The Away adjustment. Teams only marks someone Away after about 5 minutes of inactivity. When a person goes from Available straight to Away (or an idle/inactive status), the last 5 minutes of that Available stretch are re-labelled as unavailable. It never reaches back past the start of the stretch the monitor saw. On timelines this shows as a striped block.
  7. Failed checks aren't guessed. If a check for someone fails, nothing is counted for them until their next successful check.
  8. Periods. Today runs from midnight on this computer's clock. Last 7 days is today plus the previous 6 days. All time is everything kept. Time crossing midnight is split between the two days. History older than 180 days is deleted automatically.

Worked example

The monitor goes live at 09:00 and Sam is Available. Sam steps away at 09:15; Teams marks Sam Away at about 09:20 and the next check sees it. Sam is Available again from 09:30. The monitor is stopped at 10:00.

Online 09:00–10:00 = 60 min · Unavailable 09:20–09:30 (10 min) + 5-minute Away adjustment (09:15–09:20) = 15 min · Available 60 − 15 = 45 min (75%).

If the page had been closed from 09:40 to 09:50, those 10 minutes would simply be missing — Online 50 min, Available 35 min — and on the timeline they'd show as an empty striped gap. Unmonitored time is never counted as Available.
